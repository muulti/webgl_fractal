Fractal Renderer

Made by Diego (Multi)

Here´s my fractal renderer project using webgl. Originally coded the shaders in unity but here they are in WebGL so anyone with a browser can play with it!.

Have in mind:

If the render doesn´t work make sure to turn on hardware acceleration in your browser settings. Unfortunately it doesn´t work on mobile devices.
