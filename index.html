Fractal Renderer

Made by Diego (Multi)

Here´s my fractal renderer project using webgl. Originally coded the shaders in unity but here they are in WebGL so anyone with a browser can play with it!

Instructions

If the render doesn´t work make sure to turn on hardware acceleration in your browser settings. Unfortunately it doesn´t work on mobile devices

Scroll to zoom in & zoom out

Drag & Drop to move around the image

Reset zoom level and position by pressing R

What is it?

This is a personal experiment that renders fractals by applying recursive functions to complex numbers. Each complex number has a real part and an imaginary part, which correspond to the vertical and horizontal axes, respectively.

By defining a maximum number of iterations, you can check whether a number diverges or converges (after applying the function that many times) relative to a defined diameter, which sets the range for convergence.

Each pixel on the screen corresponds to a complex number, and by measuring its divergence, you can assign it a color accordingly. This process gives rise to diverse, beautiful, and infinitely intricate fractal sets.

Be careful! Fractals are infinitely complex. This means that while you keep zooming, new shapes and configurations keep appearing. However, an issue also appears. Float variables have a finite precision therefore inevitably rendering a pixelated image when zoomed in too much.
